A travel memory I won't forget

The Day We Were Robbed in Athens

A confusing question, a crowded metro, and a few tense seconds that changed how I think about staying alert while travelling.

Read my story ↓

What happened

One of the most frightening moments of our trip to Greece happened in the metro. We were standing with my parents when two people approached us and asked for directions to the airport. Their English was really bad, so at first, it was difficult to understand exactly what they were asking.

We all looked up and tried to help them, explaining that they needed to go back to the previous station and change lines. My mother was completely sure that she had closed the zipper of her bag, but a few seconds later, she looked down and realized that it was open. She immediately told me that her wallet was gone. Everything happened so quickly.

My dad and I surrounded the two people while trying to figure out what had happened. Then, suddenly, I saw one of them drop my mother's wallet onto the floor.

At that moment, I realized that the question about the metro had probably been a distraction while they were trying to steal from her bag. We got the wallet back, but it was an incredibly tense moment.

Even though we were lucky that nothing important was lost, that experience taught me to be much more careful in crowded places, especially when strangers suddenly approach you and try to distract you.

What I learned

A reminder for other travellers

Stay aware of your belongings in crowded places. If someone unexpectedly distracts you, take a moment to check that your bags are still secure.
